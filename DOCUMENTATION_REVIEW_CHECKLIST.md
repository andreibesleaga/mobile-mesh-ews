# Documentation Review Checklist

Use this checklist before merging a public-facing documentation change.

- [ ] The status of the claim is clear: fact, proposal, assumption, target, or verified result.
- [ ] The document does not imply operational alerting, public-authority approval, autonomous physical action, or live data processing.
- [ ] Safety, privacy, security, accessibility, jurisdiction, and dual-use effects were considered.
- [ ] Quantitative claims identify test conditions, evidence, version, date, limitations, and reviewer; otherwise they are labelled targets.
- [ ] Standard, regulatory, and provider claims link to authoritative sources and do not overstate conformance or affiliation.
- [ ] Links are relative where they point inside the repository, and headings are unique and descriptive.
- [ ] Requirements, risks, decisions, and references are updated when the design changes.
- [ ] Examples contain no secrets, personal data, copyrighted material without permission, or instructions enabling unauthorised warning or control.
