# Code of Conduct

Participants must be respectful, evidence-led, and mindful that early-warning technologies can affect safety, privacy, and vulnerable communities. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, bad-faith disruption, or contributions intended for surveillance, targeting, or harm are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's reporting mechanisms. Reports should avoid unnecessary personal data and will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect participants and the project boundary.
